

*This document defines the permanent brand architecture of the INSAN Healthcare Ecosystem. It should rarely change.*

**Status:** Source of Truth (Official Project Blueprint)
**Purpose:** المرجع الأساسي والرسمي لهيكلية العلامة التجارية لمنظومة الرعاية الصحية. يُمنع تكرار هذه الهيكلية في أي ملف آخر، ويتم الاكتفاء بالإشارة إلى هذا الملف لضمان مركزية التحديث.

---

## 1. Vision

INSAN is building a unified Egyptian Healthcare Ecosystem that connects hospitals, medical centers, healthcare professionals, and patients under one trusted platform.

The long-term vision is to become the leading healthcare platform in Egypt by developing, operating, and transforming healthcare institutions through one unified operational and branding system.

---

## 2. Business Model

INSAN is a Healthcare Platform.

The platform may:
- Manage hospitals.
- Operate medical centers.
- Build new healthcare brands.
- Partner with existing healthcare institutions.
- Provide operational consulting.
- Develop healthcare systems.
- Launch new medical centers.
- Expand into future healthcare services.

Hospitals are only one part of the INSAN ecosystem.

---

## 3. Ownership Model

INSAN is the Master Brand.

Hospitals operate under INSAN.

Medical Centers operate under hospitals while following INSAN standards.

Every entity maintains its own operational identity while sharing one unified strategic identity.

---

## 4. Brand Hierarchy

- **Master Brand:** INSAN (Egyptian Healthcare Platform)
- **Sub-Brands:** Hospitals 
- **Signature Brands:** Medical Centers 

*Note: Medical Centers may exist:*
- Inside hospitals.
- As standalone facilities.
- As future INSAN-operated healthcare entities.

---

    │

**Official Medical Centers (Signature Brands):**
1. مركز القلب والباطنة وإحالة الرعايات الحرجة

3. مركز مناظير الجهاز الهضمي والكبد
4. مركز الجراحات العامة والمناظير
5. مركز القدم السكري والأوعية الدموية
6. مركز علاج الألم والتدخلات المحدودة
7. مركز صحة المرأة والطفل
8. مركز الأسنان للكبار والأطفال وذوي الهمم
9. مركز الأنف والأذن وجراحات الرقبة والرأس
10. مركز العظام وإصابات الملاعب

**Standard Center Structure:**
كل مركز طبي يجب أن يتكون تشغيلياً وتسويقياً من:
- مميزات المركز (Center Features)
- خدمات المركز (Center Services)
- العيادات التخصصية (Specialized Clinics)
- جدول العيادات (Clinics Schedule)

## 8. Naming Convention

All new healthcare entities must follow the INSAN naming philosophy.

Examples:
- INSAN Orthopedic Center
- INSAN Cardiac Center
- INSAN Women's Health Center
- Future Hospital
- Delta International Hospital

Future healthcare entities should maintain naming consistency across the ecosystem.

## 9. Growth Philosophy

The architecture is designed for unlimited expansion.

New hospitals can be added.
New medical centers can be added.
New healthcare companies can be added.
New services can be added.
Without changing the core architecture.
