# Business Knowledge Base

> INSAN Healthcare Ecosystem
>
> Permanent Institutional Knowledge

---

## Purpose

This folder is the official Knowledge Base of the INSAN Healthcare project.

It stores the Source of Truth for every healthcare service, department and healthcare program.

These documents are not campaign documents.

They are long-term institutional knowledge assets.

---

## Usage

Future AI Workers, Campaign Cards, Content Strategy, Creative Direction, Website Content and Marketing Campaigns should use these files as their primary reference.

---

## Structure

```
knowledge/
├── README.md
├── services/
├── programs/
└── departments/
```

---

## Principle

Every healthcare program, service and department should have one official document.

That document becomes the permanent memory of that entity.

All future decisions should be consistent with the relevant knowledge document.
