# Service Overview

## Service Name

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Unit العناية المركزة

---

## Service Definition

The Intensive Care Unit is an organized critical care system designed to provide continuous, coordinated and multidisciplinary care for critically ill patients.

The ICU is not merely a hospital department with beds.

It is a complete care system that integrates medical expertise, family communication, physician partnership, financial transparency and operational excellence into one organized critical care experience.

The ICU operates within the broader INSAN Healthcare Ecosystem, delivered through Delta International Hospital and the Future Specialized Hospital.

INSAN drives the continuous development, governance, operational improvements and service philosophy across all ICU operations.

---

# Vision

To become one of the leading critical care systems in Egypt by delivering organized, transparent and human-centered intensive care that earns the trust of patients, families, physicians and investors alike.

---

# Mission

Provide critically ill patients with coordinated multidisciplinary care while maintaining transparent communication with families, trusted partnerships with referring physicians and sustainable operations for investors — all within a system built on trust, organization and respect.

---

# Why This Service Exists

Critical care is one of the most frightening experiences a family can face.

When a loved one is admitted to the ICU, families enter a world of uncertainty.

They do not know what is happening.

They do not know who is making decisions.

They do not know whether the care is organized or chaotic.

They often feel excluded, uninformed and powerless.

Referring physicians face their own frustrations.

They send patients to the ICU and lose visibility.

Communication gaps emerge.

Follow-up becomes uncertain.

The referral relationship weakens.

Investors see the ICU as a revenue driver, but often without the operational clarity needed to sustain it.

The ICU service exists to solve these problems — not through promises, but through organization.

Organized critical care.

Organized communication.

Organized transitions.

Organized trust.

The ICU system was designed to transform the critical care experience from one of fear and confusion into one of confidence, transparency and coordinated care.
